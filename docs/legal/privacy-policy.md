## 1. Scope and provider

This policy applies to the Simple Fasting mobile app, simplefasting.app, and messages sent to Simple Fasting support. “Simple Fasting,” “we,” and “us” mean the developer or legal provider identified in the applicable app-store listing.

The current app is local-first, requires no account, and has no Simple Fasting cloud backend. This policy does not apply to app stores, operating systems, email providers, hosting providers, export destinations, or third-party websites, which operate under their own policies.

## 2. Information stored in the app

The app stores the information needed to provide its features locally on your device:

- Active and completed fasting sessions, including dates, start and end times, and durations.
- Fasting goals and optional notes you enter.
- Reminder, appearance, accent, and widget preferences.
- Statistics, charts, streaks, and heatmaps derived locally from fasting history.
- Local storage metadata needed to validate and maintain app data.
- A limited local diagnostic log when storage initialization, reminder restoration, or app rendering fails.

This information is used on your device to operate the timer, history, statistics, local notifications, supported widgets, settings, and exports. We do not intentionally receive this local app information.

Depending on your operating-system and device-backup settings, Apple, Google, or your device provider may include app data in a device backup. We do not control those backups.

## 3. No accounts, tracking, advertising, or sale

The current app does not provide free or paid accounts. It does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, cross-app tracking, behavioral profiling, or a Simple Fasting backend.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising or targeted advertising. We do not use local fasting information to advertise to you, determine insurance or employment eligibility, or build a profile about you.

Because the app does not send local fasting information to us, we cannot view, recover, remotely delete, or respond to access requests for data that remains only on your device.

## 4. Website and technical delivery data

The website is designed as static HTML and CSS and does not intentionally use analytics cookies, advertising pixels, fingerprinting, or account cookies.

Hosting, content-delivery, security, and network providers necessarily process ordinary request data to deliver and protect the website. This can include IP address, user agent, requested URL, referring URL, timestamps, approximate region derived from an IP address, and security or error logs. Those providers may retain logs under their own terms and security policies.

We use this technical data only as needed to deliver, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the website, comply with law, and prevent abuse. We do not use it to track activity across unrelated websites or apps.

## 5. Emails and support

If you email support or report a bug, we and the relevant email providers process your email address, message, attachments, and other information you choose to send. We use it to respond, investigate issues, provide support, protect the service, and keep records when reasonably necessary.

Email is not a secure channel for sensitive health information. Do not send fasting history, medical records, passwords, government identifiers, payment information, or other information that is not needed to resolve your request.

Support messages are kept only as long as reasonably needed for the request, security, legal obligations, and legitimate recordkeeping, then deleted or de-identified where practicable. You may ask us to delete a support conversation, subject to legal and security retention requirements.

## 6. Permissions, notifications, widgets, exports, and diagnostics

Notification permission is requested only when you enable reminders. Notifications are scheduled locally through the operating system. Supported widgets receive the limited local fasting state needed to display them.

You can change notification permissions in device settings and reminder or widget preferences in the app. Denying optional permissions may disable the related feature but does not prevent basic local fasting tracking.

Exports are created only when you request them. An export leaves the app when you choose a destination through the operating-system share sheet. You are responsible for the destination, recipients, security, and later deletion of exported files.

When certain app errors occur, Simple Fasting may keep up to 50 recent diagnostic events in local app storage. A diagnostic event can include its time and category, a sanitized error name and message, limited component context, app version, build version, and platform. The diagnostic report is designed not to include fasting history, goals, notes, settings, account identifiers, advertising identifiers, or device identifiers. Potential email addresses, web addresses, and local file paths in error text are redacted where recognized, but you should still review a report before sharing it.

Diagnostic events are not analytics and are not automatically transmitted. They remain on the device until they are removed by the rolling limit, app-data clearing, or uninstall behavior. A report leaves the app only when you choose the diagnostic option inside Report bug and select a destination in the operating-system share sheet. If you attach it to an email, the email practices in Section 5 apply.

## 7. Purposes and legal grounds

Where data-protection law requires a legal ground, we process website request data and support messages as necessary to provide requested services, communicate with you, secure and maintain the service, comply with legal obligations, and pursue legitimate interests that do not override your rights. Where consent is required, we will request it separately.

We do not claim that local fasting information is anonymous merely because it stays on a device. It may be sensitive to you, and anyone with access to your unlocked device, backups, widgets, notifications, or exports may be able to see it.

## 8. Retention, deletion, and your controls

You control information stored by the app and can:

- Edit or delete completed fasting sessions.
- Export app data in JSON or CSV format.
- Clear local app data from Settings.
- Remove app data by uninstalling the app, subject to operating-system backups and retention behavior.
- Disable notifications or remove widgets through app or device settings.
- Review and explicitly share a local diagnostic report; clearing app data also clears local diagnostics.

Clearing or uninstalling may be irreversible. Create and safely store an export first if you need a copy. We cannot restore information that was never transmitted to us.

## 9. Privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information we actually hold, such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent, or appeal. You may also have the right to complain to a local privacy or data-protection authority.

Send a request to support@simplefasting.app. We may need to verify the request and may retain limited information where permitted or required by law. These rights do not let us access or delete local app data that we do not possess.

Residents of U.S. states with applicable privacy laws may also request information about collection, use, disclosure, sale, or sharing. For the current service, we do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not offer a financial incentive for personal information.

## 10. Security and incidents

We use a local-first design and reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information we handle. No device, email system, network, or storage method is completely secure. Protect your device with system access controls, keep it updated, review notification and widget visibility, and store exports securely.

If an incident involving information held by us requires notification under applicable law, we will provide the required notices. A compromise of your device, personal backup, email account, or chosen export destination is generally outside our control.

## 11. Children and health-related information

Simple Fasting is not directed to children under 13 in the United States or the equivalent minimum age in another jurisdiction, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child sent information to support, contact us so we can review and delete it where required.

Fasting may be inappropriate for children and adolescents. Anyone under the age of legal majority should use the app only with involvement from a parent or guardian and guidance from a qualified healthcare professional.

Simple Fasting is a general wellness tracking tool, not a healthcare provider, insurer, medical-record service, or substitute for professional care. Do not send medical records through support email.

## 12. International processing

Website and email providers may process technical or support information in countries other than your own. Those countries may have different privacy laws. Where required, providers and we must use recognized safeguards for international transfers.

Local app data remains subject to your device, platform, backup, and export choices and is not intentionally transferred to us.

## 13. Future accounts, paid features, and integrations

We may add optional accounts, synchronization, paid features, health integrations, or other functionality in a future release. Those features are not part of the current app.

Before a new feature begins collecting or transmitting information in a materially different way, we will update this policy, describe the relevant data and purposes, and request permission or consent where required. A future free tier will not be treated as permission for advertising tracking or sale of personal information. Paid functionality may require separate payment-provider and account disclosures.

We will not silently turn existing local-only fasting information into remotely collected information.

## 14. Changes and contact

We may update this policy to reflect product, legal, security, or operational changes. We will publish a new version and effective date. If a change is material, we will provide additional notice in the app, on the website, or through another appropriate channel before or when the change takes effect where reasonably possible or legally required.

Continued use after an effective date is subject to the updated policy, but consent will be requested separately when law requires it. Earlier app releases retain the policy copy bundled with that release unless updated.

Privacy, support, and rights requests can be sent to support@simplefasting.app. Bug reports can be sent to bugs@simplefasting.app.
